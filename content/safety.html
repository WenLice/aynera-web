Safety centre

Your safety matters .. here and offline.

From first chat to offline dates, Focus, and Together .. safety tools stay free. Serious reports get human review. If you’re in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

What we build in

Verification before discovery

Phone, email, and liveness checks before anyone appears in introductions. Badges explain what was verified .. they raise trust, they never guarantee behaviour.

Recommended public places

For first meets, prefer busy public spots and partner venues where available .. places with people around, clear hours, and a safer vibe than private or isolated spots. These are recommendations, not surveillance. You still choose where to go.

Date plan & optional check-in

Set venue and time before you go. Check in when you arrive if you want .. so your plan is clear and a trusted person can know you’re okay. No continuous live tracking. No forced location trail.

Share Date with someone you trust

One tap sends venue and time to a friend or family member. They know where you planned to be .. without Aynera following your every move. Built for Meet, useful anytime you go offline.

Report & block .. in-app or after

Report a profile, message, or offline incident in a few taps. Blocking ends contact immediately. You can still report after a date .. safety does not end when the chat ends.

Safety across every chapter

Focus and Together pause discovery .. they never remove your exits. Report, block, respectful close, and cooling-off stay available through Era space too. Commitment should feel safer, not locked in.

Human review when it matters

Automated filters help triage. Trained people handle severe cases, appeals, underage concerns, and offline safety reports .. so serious issues are not left to a bot alone.

Safety at Aynera Gatherings

A gathering is published only when host, venue, capacity and a safety owner are real. Verification raises confidence; it never guarantees behaviour.

Host presence

A visible host, public venue, conduct expectations, and an easy report route during the gathering.

Consent and contact

Photo consent before pictures. No forced pairings. No forced contact-sharing. Harassment and unwanted repeated approaches can be reported.

After the gathering

Event removal when needed. Post-event reporting stays open. No continuous live tracking. Trusted-person sharing stays optional.
