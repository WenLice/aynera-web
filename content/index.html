Why Aynera exists

More people is not the same as more possibility

Your everyday circle only takes you so far .. dating apps can show you more people than ever, but more profiles do not always create better opportunities

Aynera expands your circle with people you may genuinely want to meet .. and gives you more natural ways to cross paths

Meet through Taste

Small social experiences around shared interests, energy and lifestyle .. giving people a more natural way to meet than starting everything with a formal date

What is Taste?

Taste is the way you actually live .. what you enjoy, how you spend your time, your social rhythm, food, movement, culture, hobbies and the little things that make an experience feel like you

Aynera uses Taste to create better context around people and experiences .. not to pretend that a percentage can predict chemistry

When you choose each other, we stop the search

If one connection starts becoming more important, Focus lets both people mutually pause new discovery and give that connection more attention
