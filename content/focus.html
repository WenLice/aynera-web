02 · Focus

Give one person your full attention.

Focus is a protected window for two people who want to explore seriously .. without new faces pulling you away. It only starts when both of you agree.

Inside this chapter

Mutual or it doesn’t exist

One person can ask to Focus. Nothing changes until the other independently accepts. No silent lock-ins. No pressure traps dressed as romance.

A clear window of time

Choose 14, 30, or 60 days. For that stretch, new introductions pause for both of you. Unrelated chats move aside so this connection can breathe without the noise of options.

Enforced in the product

Focus is not a soft status badge. Eligibility for new intros and likes is paused for both accounts. You get the calm of commitment-in-progress .. without pretending exclusivity yet.

Decide with dignity

Extend Focus, step into Together, return to discovery, or close with care. Each person chooses privately. Ending Focus is allowed anytime .. respect stays the rule.
