01 · Meet

Meet people worth knowing.

A few thoughtful 1:1 introductions. Limited, explained, and meant to become real conversation — without an endless feed.

Inside this chapter

Limited introductions with a plain-language reason they may be worth your attention. Intent, location, Taste and reciprocal eligibility can inform who you see. We will not display a compatibility percentage.

Fewer introductions on purpose

In the pilot you get a small set of thoughtful intros. Scarcity protects attention .. so you actually read someone, instead of training your brain to discard faces in under a second.

A reason you might connect

Every introduction comes with plain-language context .. pace, intent, distance, shared interests. No fake percentage. No mystery algorithm flex. Just enough signal to start well.

Conversation before contact

Conversation opens through controlled, mutual interaction — not unlimited cold inbound messaging. Share numbers and socials only when it feels right.

Meet safely, online and off

Prefer recommended public places and partner venues where available. Use a date plan, optional check-in, and Share Date with someone you trust .. see the Safety centre.
