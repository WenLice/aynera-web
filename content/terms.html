13. Product chapters

Meet → Focus → Together → Era space, with Era Ahead as the longer path.

Meet

Curated introductions, conversation, and safer public-meet practices.

Focus

Mutual Focus is intended to begin only when both people confirm independently.

Together

Together is intended to stop discovery for both people when both choose exclusivity on Aynera. It does not create a legal marriage or partnership.

Era space

A private shared space after discovery ends. Content there remains subject to these Terms and the Privacy Notice.

Era Ahead

Longer-horizon product direction. Features may ship later or change.
