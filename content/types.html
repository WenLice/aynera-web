Types

One founding network.

The current founding community is one Aynera network. Specialised cohorts, including Professionals, are future or limited experiments — not a first-visit choice, and not a ranking of worth.

One Aynera community first Same safety tools Not an income or prestige club

Kept for compatibility

Join the founding circle first. Context-specific cohorts can wait.

Do not use income, employer prestige or education pedigree as a reason to join. If a specialised cohort is tested later, it will be a context-specific group — not “better users.”

Aynera Core

Verified introductions for people who want something real.

For adults seeking an intentional relationship in a local founding network. Delhi NCR is opening first. You complete identity and liveness checks, build a thoughtful profile, and pass human review before discovery.

You need · Age 18+, local cohort, verification, reviewed profile, clear intent

Aynera Professionals

A smaller, manually admitted cohort with professional context.

For established professionals who want career-stage and lifestyle context in introductions. You meet every Core requirement, verify workplace or professional activity, and complete a short professional profile for manual review.

Not · An income club, net-worth filter, prestige ranking, or matrimony service

What stays equal

Safety and mutuality never depend on type.

Reporting, blocking, date-safety tools, and mutual Focus / Together rules apply the same way for every member. Employer prestige and verification badges never guarantee behaviour.

You get · One account, one journey language, equal protection

Honest admission

We open when the local cohort is ready.

If a balanced cohort isn’t available yet, you wait. Aynera will not invent urgency or put you in an empty marketplace dressed up as choice.
