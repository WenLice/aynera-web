Verify before you appear.

Real adults. Clear intent. No discovery until checks pass.

How we deliver this

Phone + email

OTP and verified email before profile approval. Rate limits stop spam and fake signup storms.

Video selfie / liveness

A short liveness check confirms you’re a real person matching your photos — not a scraped gallery.

Age assurance

18+ only. Uncertain cases go to manual review. Minors never enter discovery.

Human review

During the pilot, every profile is reviewed for quality, authenticity, and policy before introductions begin.

Verification raises trust. It never guarantees perfect behaviour — report and block stay free.
