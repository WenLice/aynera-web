How it works

Join a local network. Meet in more than one way.

Not every Aynera session has to end in Focus or Together. You may simply meet new people, exchange contact details, return next week, or leave after finding someone.

No endless feed No unilateral relationship mode No fake urgency

The use path

Five steps through a living local network.

Join when there is enough relevant local activity. Discover through 1:1 introductions and/or small Taste gatherings. Keep exploring if nothing clicks — Focus and Together stay optional.

1. Join a local network

Start where there are enough people nearby.

Choose the founding market, share what you are open to, and join when there is enough relevant local activity. Delhi is opening first. Mumbai and Bangalore are next-city interest until each is ready.

You get · Honest waitlists and no fake countdowns

2. Build context

Profile, Taste and intent — with clear privacy.

Review, profile, Taste and intent help Aynera understand how you like to meet. We explain what is public, what stays private, and what is used only for recommendations.

You get · Context without a compatibility score

How intent works →

3. Discover your way

1:1 introductions and/or small Taste gatherings.

Receive curated introductions, see relevant small gatherings, or both. The gathering is an activation of the same local network — not a separate events product.

You get · More than one way to cross paths

1:1 Meet →  ·  Taste Gatherings →

4. Meet safely

Talk here first .. meet when it feels right.

For 1:1, chat in-app and use public-place / date-plan tools. Gathering participants follow host and community rules. Safety tools stay free.

You get · Control before, during, and after the offline step

Explore the Safety centre →

5. Keep exploring or focus

The network refreshes. Mutual states stay optional.

If nothing clicks, new people and opportunities appear. If one connection does, Focus and Together remain optional mutual states — not a required journey.

You get · A reason to return without infinite swiping

 ·  Focus →  ·  Together →

The rule underneath every step

If it isn’t mutual, nothing changes.

A request never becomes a relationship state by itself. Focus and Together begin only after both people choose them independently .. and either person can still leave respectfully.
